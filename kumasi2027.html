Planning for 2027

Africa Climate Week 2027 | Kumasi

In 2027, OMEF is working toward a major convening in Kumasi, Ghana—bringing community knowledge, traditional leadership, policy, philanthropy, enterprise, and technology into the same room.

Register Interest Explore Event Partnership

Convening Purpose

“This is not just more talk. This is about relationships and practical pathways to delivery.”

Some challenges are too large for any one institution to solve alone. Our convenings create space for community knowledge, traditional leadership, policy, philanthropy, research, media and responsible enterprise to meet around practical outcomes.

We're not aiming to host conversation for its own sake — we're aiming to help relationships form, ideas sharpen, and real pathways toward delivery emerge. Rooted in Kumasi, the programme will explore how African leadership, knowledge, enterprise and culture can shape practical responses to climate and development priorities.

Dates, venue, host arrangements and programme commitments will be announced once formally confirmed.

Convening Agenda

Programme Themes

Seven strategic tracks bringing stakeholders together around practical delivery and long-term resilience:

Theme 01

Nature and natural capital

Biodiversity, landscape restoration, water, land, the systems that sustain life.

Theme 02

Climate resilience and livelihoods

Food systems, community resilience, enterprise, just pathways to prosperity.

Theme 03

Energy and infrastructure

Responsible transition, access, cities, resilient infrastructure.

Theme 04

Sustainable finance and policy

How public, philanthropic and private capital can back credible outcomes.

Theme 05

Technology and responsible AI

Tools that serve people, institutions and transparent delivery.

Theme 06

Small business and supply chains

Entrepreneurs, producers, regional value chains.

Theme 07

Culture, youth and future generations

Leadership, imagination, knowledge carried across generations.
