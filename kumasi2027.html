Planning for 2027 · Kumasi, Ghana

Africa Climate Week 2027 | Kumasi

In 2027, OMEF is working toward a major convening in Kumasi, Ghana—bringing community knowledge, traditional leadership, policy, philanthropy, enterprise, and technology into the same room.

Home Kumasi 2027

“This is not just more talk. This is about relationships and practical pathways to delivery.”

Rooted in Kumasi, the programme will explore how African leadership, knowledge, enterprise, and culture can shape practical responses to climate and development priorities. Dates, venue, host arrangements, and programme commitments will be published as soon as formally confirmed.

Programmatic Tracks

Seven Central Programme Themes

Curated dialogue and delivery tracks addressing critical regional and global ecological priorities

Theme 01

Nature & Natural Capital

Biodiversity, landscape restoration, water, land, and the living systems that sustain life across the continent.

Theme 02

Climate Resilience & Livelihoods

Food systems, community resilience, local small enterprise, and just, dignified pathways to prosperity.

Theme 03

Energy & Infrastructure

Responsible transition, energy access, resilient African cities, and sustainable community-level infrastructure.

Theme 04

Sustainable Finance & Policy

How public, philanthropic, and private capital can transparently back credible, evidenced outcomes.

Theme 05

Technology & Responsible AI

Technological tools, cybersecurity, and ethical artificial intelligence that serve people, institutions, and transparent delivery.

Theme 06

Small Business & Supply Chains

Entrepreneurs, agricultural producers, regional value chains, and verified transparent trade.

Theme 07

Culture, Youth & Generations

Youth leadership, indigenous wisdom, cultural imagination, and ancestral knowledge carried forward across generations.
