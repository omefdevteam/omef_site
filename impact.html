Measurement & Transparency

Impact

Impact is about what changes because of our work: healthier ecosystems, stronger communities, greater opportunity, and a stronger capacity to respond to what lies ahead.

We are developing an impact approach that links environmental, social, educational and institutional impacts, and we will always be transparent about what is verified, what is formally targeted and what remains a longer-term aspiration.

Holistic Framework

Four Dimensions of Sustainable Change

Our impact framework links environmental, social, educational, and institutional outcomes across four interdependent spheres:

Nature

Land, water, biodiversity, ecosystem resilience. Safeguarding watersheds, recovering degraded soils, and bolstering living natural infrastructure.

People

Livelihoods, wellbeing, participation, dignity, and opportunity. Ensuring community members lead and thrive through ecological restoration.

Future

Learning, digital capability, leadership, intergenerational benefit. Empowering youth to build skills, enterprise, and confidence for what comes next.

Systems

Institutions, methodologies, partnerships, policy, long-term stewardship. Building the governance and institutional strength to make change hold.

Across all four: gender and inclusion, community rights and participation, safeguards and do-no-harm practice, local ownership, transparent evidence and a real habit of learning and adapting.

Measurement Discipline

How we count our numbers

We use four labels consistently, so no one has to guess what a figure means:

Verified Progress

Achieved Outcome

An achieved result, backed by an identified source and reporting period, with the verifier named where independent verification applies.

Approved Target

Endorsed Target

A formally endorsed future result, with a target date, baseline, owner, and a funded or defined delivery pathway.

Long-term ambition

Directional Scale

A directional aspiration that communicates scale without implying it's already committed or achieved.

External Reference Point

Contextual Evidence

Evidence from another organisation or comparable model, shown for context. Never counted as OMEF impact.

Reporting Status: “Our programmes are moving from design to delivery. Verified results will be posted here as baselines, and reporting is completed.”
Grounded Perspective

Stories of Impact

Numbers give us scale. Stories teach us what that means. Our impact stories tell the stories of people as decision makers, practitioners, learners and leaders with real context, credit to the partners involved and care not to let one person stand in for an entire community.

“The knowledge that is in the communities is not an add-on to the work. It is the foundation on which the work has to be built.”

Open Accountability

Reporting and Accountability

As the programmes evolve, we will report simply and proportionately on progress against the agreed indicators, significant changes, the use of funds (where relevant), safeguarding and grievance information and what we have learned as we go along.
