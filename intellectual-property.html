Intellectual Property & Copyright Notice

Organisation: Our Mother Earth Foundation (OMEF) Effective Date: 1st September 2026 Policy Type: Copyright & Trademark Protection

Our Mother Earth Foundation ("OMEF") supports the responsible sharing of knowledge, research and information concerning environmental protection, climate action and sustainable development.

At the same time, OMEF seeks to protect the intellectual property rights associated with material that it creates, commissions or lawfully uses.

1. OMEF material

Unless otherwise stated, original material published by OMEF on this Website is owned by or licensed to OMEF.

This may include:

  • written content;
  • reports and publications;
  • research and analysis;
  • photographs;
  • videos;
  • graphics and illustrations;
  • website design and content;
  • logos and branding;
  • educational materials; and
  • other original works.

2. Responsible use

You may access, read and share links to OMEF's publicly available material.

Reasonable extracts may be reproduced for legitimate educational, academic, research, journalistic or non-commercial purposes provided that:

  • the material is accurately reproduced;
  • OMEF is clearly acknowledged as the source;
  • the original source is identified;
  • the material is not presented in a misleading context; and
  • reproduction does not imply endorsement by OMEF.

3. Commercial use

Commercial reproduction, substantial republication, adaptation, modification, distribution or exploitation of OMEF-owned material requires prior written permission unless expressly permitted by an applicable licence.

Requests for permission should be sent to: enquiries@ourmotherearthfoundation.org

4. Third-party material

Some material appearing on the Website may belong to third parties, including photographs, logos, quotations, publications, datasets or other content.

Third-party material remains subject to the rights of its respective owner. Permission to use third-party material must be obtained from the relevant rights holder where required.

5. OMEF name and logo

The name Our Mother Earth Foundation, the abbreviation OMEF, and associated logos, emblems, and branding may not be used in a way that falsely suggests endorsement, sponsorship, affiliation or partnership with OMEF.

Official high-resolution brand assets for accredited media and approved partners can be accessed in our Media Centre.

6. Copyright concerns

If you believe material appearing on the Website infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, please contact us with sufficient information to identify the material and explain the basis of your concern.

We will consider legitimate notices and take appropriate action where necessary.

Direct intellectual property notices to: legal@ourmotherearthfoundation.org

7. Attribution

Where OMEF material is reproduced with permission, appropriate attribution should normally identify:

Our Mother Earth Foundation (OMEF), and where applicable, the document title and original publication date or URL source.
