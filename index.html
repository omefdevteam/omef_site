OUR MOTHER EARTH FOUNDATION · PEOPLE. NATURE. FUTURE.

What we protect today is the start of a better future.

“A young person given a real opportunity can move a whole generation forward.”

We believe healthy ecosystems and thriving communities are inseparable. Restoring a river strengthens the community around it. When we care for nature, we are building something communities can stand on: resilience, dignity, and hope.

We partner with communities, governments, leaders, educators, and partners to help shape the initiatives that restore landscapes, strengthen livelihoods, expand opportunity, and build a future worth inheriting. Because caring for Mother Earth is really about caring for each other.

Universal Shared Truth

We Have Only One Mother Earth

There are few truths that are true in all cultures, all countries and all generations. We all want to see our children succeed. We all rely on healthy land, clean water and communities that can handle anything that comes. And we all have some responsibility in the future we leave.

Our Mother Earth Foundation is here to bring people together around those shared values and to turn those into action that actually lasts beyond a single project, beyond a single event.

Integrated initiatives combining ecological restoration, water stewardship, local knowledge, education, and institutional capability.

Programme Vision in Development Ecological Restoration

Tano River: A Vision

A river carries more than water; it carries memory, livelihood, and generations of connection. The Tano River vision brings ecological restoration, water stewardship, sustainable livelihoods, and local knowledge into one long-term program, built to create healthier landscapes and stronger futures together.

The Oath

“To help create a healthier river landscape that supports biodiversity, resilience and dignified opportunity for the generations living along it today and those yet to come.”

Discover Tano River
Locally Anchored Education Digital Capability

Asanteman Digital Future

Every generation deserves a hand in shaping what comes next. Asanteman Digital Future is a vision for locally anchored education—digital trust, cybersecurity, AI, entrepreneurship, and leadership—built to remain within Asanteman and grow through its own institutions, educators, and young people.

The Pledge

“To help build channels for the prosperity of knowledge, credentials, institutions and opportunity in Asanteman and for generations to come.”

Explore Asanteman Digital Future
Planning for 2027 · Kumasi, Ghana

Africa Climate Week 2027 | Kumasi

In 2027, OMEF is working toward a major convening in Kumasi, Ghana—bringing community knowledge, traditional leadership, policy, philanthropy, enterprise, and technology into the same room. This is not just more talk. This is about relationships and practical pathways to delivery. Dates, venue and program details will be published as soon as formally confirmed.
