Rigorous Methodologies

Methodology Development Lifecycle

Methodologies give us a common way of defining activities, safeguards, evidence, and outcomes. We endorse development where on-the-ground experience translates into something transparent and repeatable.

1. In Development Research, consultation or drafting underway
2. Submitted Formally provided to a named body
3. Under Review Review confirmed by receiving body
4. Approved Formal approval available and linked
