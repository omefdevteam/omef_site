Cookies & Online Tracking Notice

Organisation: Our Mother Earth Foundation (OMEF) Effective Date: 1st September 2026 Status: Active & Audited

This notice explains how Our Mother Earth Foundation ("OMEF", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. We believe in complete transparency regarding how digital tools and technical assets function across our web platforms.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that are stored on, or accessed from, your device when you visit a website.

Similar technologies may include tracking pixels, scripts, tags, local storage and other technologies that store or access information on a device.

2. Why we use these technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes including:

Current Operational Reality & Audit Confirmation:

This website currently does not use any non-essential cookies, behavioral tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party web analytics cookies (such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel). The only external network connections made by the site are strictly necessary for rendering typography and iconography via trusted content delivery networks (Cloudflare CDN for FontAwesome icons and Google Fonts for typography) and platform-level infrastructure for DDoS security and edge caching.

Strictly necessary functions

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to operate, maintain security, remember essential settings or provide functionality you have requested.

Website analytics

Where enabled in the future, analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors use the website and improve its content, performance and usability. Currently, non-essential analytics tracking is not active.

Preferences

Where applicable, technologies may be used to remember choices you make when using the website.

Other technologies

If we introduce advertising, marketing, social-media tracking or other non-essential technologies, we will update this Notice and, where required, obtain your prior consent before using them.

3. Consent

Where applicable law requires consent, we will ask for your consent before placing or using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can change or withdraw your choices through our cookie-management mechanism where one is provided.

Important Legal Rule: Continuing to browse the website does not, by itself, constitute consent where consent is legally required. Consent must always be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous.

4. Third-party technologies

Some functions and digital assets may be provided by third-party service providers (such as content delivery networks and font providers).

Where third-party technologies are used, the relevant provider may process technical connection information (such as your IP address) in accordance with its own privacy documentation to deliver the requested asset securely.

We seek to ensure that our use of such technologies is consistent with applicable data protection and electronic-privacy requirements.

5. Browser controls

You can use your web browser settings to block, delete, or restrict cookies and local storage on your device. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • view the cookies stored on your device and delete them individually or collectively;
  • block third-party cookies or cookies from specific websites; and
  • configure your browser to block all cookies automatically.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary technologies may affect the operation or visual rendering of some parts of the website.

6. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Notice when our website technologies change or when applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page with its effective date.
